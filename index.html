🧭 Tenali Learning Guide

Teaching Method for Ages 4–5 (Concrete to Representational Addition):

In Flower Addition II, children see arithmetic visually represented as two sets of objects combining into one unified total group:

🌸 [Group 1] shows the first set of flowers.
🌸 [Group 2] shows the added set of flowers.
🌸 [Combined Group] shows both groups united into the complete sum!
🌸 Answer Buttons: Child matches the combined total with the flower-shaped number button!

Tactile & Positive: Children can tap flowers anytime to hear counting sounds. Incorrect choices trigger gentle encouragement with zero penalties. Tap "💡 Count with Me" anytime for animated guided counting across the entire equation!
